## Boundaries and corrections

Faces are anonymous generated stand-ins, not verified professional likenesses. Generated emblems, flags, team/event names and dates are illustrative and are not official or authoritative assets. In particular, the storyboard mixes example identities between phases; a real implementation must keep each lane's actual predetermined offer identical through landing and settled play. Starting headers must not leak final identities. The phone opening study suggests lateral blur; production direction remains vertical travel inside stacked windows. Reroll during opening must remain disabled even where a mockup's visual treatment is ambiguous. The implementation brief takes precedence.

These images demonstrate composition and atmosphere only. They do not validate contrast, accessibility, responsive geometry, sound, animation performance or game correctness. Production cannot use these baked mockups as interface controls, fabricated roster portraits or substitute team logos.
