# Welcome to Major Mayhem discussions 👋

Major Mayhem is a fan-made Counter-Strike draft game: open cases of real Major rosters, draft a five-player dream team (plus a coach and a bench player), then take it through a simulated Major with map vetoes, knife rounds and halftime swaps.

**Play:** https://nanox333.github.io/major-mayhem/

This is a one-person project (I'm [@nanox333](https://github.com/nanox333)), built with a lot of help from Claude Code. That makes feedback from players especially valuable. Here's where things go:

- **Ideas:** new modes, mechanics and quality-of-life. The *Ideas* form asks for a moment in a run where your idea would help.
- **Show and tell:** paste your daily share text or duel link. Best pick, worst pick, the clutch that won it.
- **Q&A:** how something works, why your team lost, where a number comes from.
- **Bugs and wrong roster data:** please use the [issue forms](https://github.com/nanox333/major-mayhem/issues/new/choose), because they ask for exactly what's needed to fix it.

The [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/nanox333/major-mayhem/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) applies here. The players in the game are real people, so keep it respectful.

GL HF!
