# Roadmap: what's coming next

v1.0.0 is out: daily challenge, map veto, knife rounds and side picks, Swiss stage, coach and bench picks, duels, *Guess the pro*, photos for 168 of 190 players and logos for all 34 teams. Full notes: [Releases](https://github.com/nanox333/major-mayhem/releases).

The next versions come from a review of the whole game. Each is a [milestone](https://github.com/nanox333/major-mayhem/milestones), and the full plan is in [docs/ROADMAP.md](https://github.com/nanox333/major-mayhem/blob/main/docs/ROADMAP.md).

1. **v1.1: Correct and trustworthy.** Scoreboards that can't show impossible numbers, real IGL and AWP roles, *Guess the pro* clues about careers rather than the dataset, narration that matches your calls, and confirming the three champion rosters still marked unverified.
2. **v1.2: Clearer decisions.** Pause and next-round controls in matches, draft cards that say what a pick will do, and a results screen that reviews the whole team.
3. **v1.3: Presentation and accessibility.** A better phone layout, a sharper desktop layout, a three-step intro, and trivia colours that don't rely on colour alone.
4. **v1.4: Retention.** A clearer way into the daily, friend challenges, and achievements for new players.

Two open questions I'd love opinions on:
- Should the daily reset at your local midnight, or at one global moment so everyone's on the same daily? (#26)
- What would you want from a leaderboard, given that fair results would need a server?

Reply here, or open an *Idea* discussion for anything bigger.
